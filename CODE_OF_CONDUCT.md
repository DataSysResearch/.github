# Code of Conduct

DataSys follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project communication.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers or organization owners. Reports will be reviewed by maintainers with appropriate confidentiality and care.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
